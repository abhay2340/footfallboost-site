Questions people ask

Are all the cakes really egg-free?

Yes. Every cake and dessert Payal bakes is 100% egg-free.

How early should I order?

For custom designs, message a few days ahead so there is time to plan the design. For something urgent, ask on WhatsApp and Payal will tell you what's possible.

Can you copy a cake from a picture?

Yes. Send a reference picture on WhatsApp and Payal will confirm the design, size and price.

How much cake do I need?

A 1 kg cake usually serves around 8–10 people. Tell Payal your guest count and she'll suggest the right size.

Where do you deliver?

Across Delhi and NCR. Pickup is also available.

Do you make hampers for Diwali, Christmas or corporate gifting?

Yes: festive hampers with chocolates, cupcakes and treats. Message with the quantity and your budget.

Got a celebration coming up?
