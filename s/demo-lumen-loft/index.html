Questions

Good to know.

How long does a full 3BHK take?

Usually 60 to 90 days from design sign-off, depending on civil work. Modular kitchens and wardrobes take about 45 days.

Do I pay before seeing the design?

No. The site visit and first 3D concept are free. You pay a booking amount only once you approve the design and quote.

Can you work with my existing furniture?

Yes. We measure what you want to keep and design the room around it.

Which areas do you cover?

Noida, Greater Noida, Gurugram, Ghaziabad and South and East Delhi.
