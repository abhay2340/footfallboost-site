Good to know

Is everything really eggless?

Yes. Every cake, bread, cookie and treat at Bake n' Flake is 100% eggless.

Do you make Jain food?

Bake n' Flake is a Jain bakery. Mention any specific Jain preferences in your order notes and Namrata will confirm.

How early should I order?

Please order 1–2 days in advance. For big or custom orders, message earlier.

Is the bakery registered?

Yes. Bake n' Flake is FSSAI registered.

Do you deliver?

Bake n' Flake is in Delhi. Share your area on WhatsApp and Namrata will confirm pickup or delivery.

How do I join a workshop?

Message on WhatsApp for upcoming bread, cake and online workshop dates.
