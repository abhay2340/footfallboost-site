Questions

How early should I book?

Please book at least 3–4 days before your date. Wedding and chandelier cakes are best booked earlier.

Do you deliver across NCR?

Yes. Mehak delivers all across Delhi/NCR.

Can you set up the cake at our venue?

For showpiece and chandelier cakes, ask about venue setup when you book.

Can you make a cake from a reference photo?

Yes. Share reference pictures on WhatsApp and Mehak will customise the design for you.

Do you make return favours and hampers?

Yes, for weddings, birthdays and festivals. Message with the quantity and occasion.

How much does a cake cost?

Every cake is customised, so the price depends on size, tiers and design. Share your details for a quote.
