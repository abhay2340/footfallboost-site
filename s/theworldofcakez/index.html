    Questions

    Can you make an eggless cake?

    Yes, eggless options are available. Just tick "Eggless" in the cake builder or mention it on WhatsApp.

    Can you copy a design from a picture?

    Yes. Send a reference picture on WhatsApp and Priya will customise it for you.

    How early should I order?

    Message a few days ahead for custom designs. For urgent orders, ask on WhatsApp and Priya will let you know what's possible.

    Do you deliver?

    Pickup is from Pratap Nagar, Mayur Vihar. Ask on WhatsApp about home delivery to your area.

    Do you make cupcakes and cookies too?

    Yes: cupcakes, cookies and other desserts, alongside cakes.

    Is Sunday open?

    The bakery is closed on Sundays. Orders for Sunday can be planned in advance. Ask Priya.
