    Questions people ask

    Can you copy a Pinterest cake?

    Reet recreates reference pictures and adapts them to your size, colours and budget. Send the link on WhatsApp.

    Can I order from another country?

    Yes. Reet manages the whole order herself, so family in Gurugram can collect it for you. Share the details on WhatsApp.

    How early should I order?

    For theme and tiered cakes, a week ahead is safest. For busy dates, book earlier.

    How does pricing work?

    Every quote is custom, based on the design, size and flavour. There is no big upfront payment.
