Whipped cream or semi-fondant?

Neha makes both. Here is how to choose.

Whipped cream cakes

  • Soft, light and creamy
  • Good for painted and piped designs
  • Best for quick celebrations at home

Semi-fondant cakes

  • Smooth finish with modelled toppers
  • Good for characters and 3D themes
  • Holds its shape for longer at a party
